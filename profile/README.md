# ObiLabs

**Self-hostable IT and security tools for organizations that want to own their data.**

ObiLabs builds administration and service-management software you run on your own
infrastructure — no per-seat tax, no vendor lock-in, and every action audited.

## What we build

- **Helios** — Google Workspace & Microsoft 365 administration. A modern portal for users,
  groups, email signatures, lifecycle automation, and a fully-audited API console.
- **Aegis** — IT service management (ITSM) built around data sovereignty and an
  append-only, auditable record.
- **MTP** — a multi-tenant portal that lets MSPs manage clients across ObiLabs products from
  one pane of glass, with cascade revocation and cross-product audit.
- **Rubric** — an open tool for authoring exam and quiz item banks from a simple DSL.

Product clients are **free and open source (AGPL)** — self-host them for as long as you like.
Optional hosting (coming soon) and MSP tooling fund the work.

## What we believe

- **Your data, your call.** Where it runs, who supports it, and who gets access are three separate
  decisions, all yours — and any access you grant, you can take back.
- **No lock-in.** Open source, self-hostable, no per-seat pricing — export and leave anytime.
- **Security and compliance, built in.** Every action is logged — who did what, when — so you can
  prove it to an auditor instead of reconstructing it.
- **Open by default.** The source is open; inspect it, extend it, trust what you can see.
